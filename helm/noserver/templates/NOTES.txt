1. Get release status:
   kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

2. Check service endpoints:
   kubectl get svc {{ include "noserver.fullname" . }}

3. Check Traefik routes:
   kubectl get ingressroutetcp,ingressrouteudp -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

4. Update ports, Traefik entrypoints, and server args in values.yaml or with --set flags for each environment.
